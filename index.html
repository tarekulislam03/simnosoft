Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Simnosoft, our products, and the early access program.

What exactly is Simnosoft?
Simnosoft is an independent B2B software company. We build focused, industry-specific business tools: Fieldhand for field-service operations (scheduling, routing, invoicing, SMS) and Inventorii for warehouse management and wholesale distribution. We are privately owned, unfunded, and have no agenda other than making our products genuinely excellent for the operators who use them.
How do Fieldhand and Inventorii work together?
Each product stands alone and serves a distinct operational area. Fieldhand is for service businesses managing trucks and technicians in the field. Inventorii is for warehouse and distribution teams managing physical inventory. If your operation includes both — for example, a pest control company that also manages a chemical supply depot — both products share a unified login, billing, and customer record system.
Do I need IT staff or technical expertise to set up?
No. Both products are built for operators, technicians, and warehouse workers with no technical background. Setup takes an afternoon. We provide one-click CSV import for your existing customers, accounts, and inventory data. Early access members get direct onboarding assistance from our team.
What does the early access 20% discount mean exactly?
When you reserve your spot, we record your email and product preference. Before public launch, you'll receive a private invitation link with the 20% discount automatically applied to your chosen subscription tier. This discount is lifetime — it applies to every future billing cycle, not just your first month. No credit card is required today.
Are there annual contracts or cancellation fees?
Never. All Simnosoft subscriptions are month-to-month. You can cancel, downgrade, or pause from your account settings at any time without penalties or exit fees. We don't use contracts as a retention mechanism — we use product quality.
Can I import my existing customer and inventory data?
Yes, easily. Both products include one-click CSV import tools for customers, accounts, job history, SKUs, and bin locations. If you're on a legacy system or are moving off spreadsheets, our onboarding team will help you prepare and validate your data import at no charge during early access.
What integrations do you support?
Fieldhand exports invoices in a format that imports cleanly into QuickBooks. Both products support CSV data export for any BI or reporting tool. We are building a native QuickBooks integration. Additional integrations are driven by operator demand — tell us what your business uses and we'll prioritize accordingly.
How is our business data protected?
All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using AES-256, hosted on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure with hourly automated backups. You own 100% of your data and can export it in full at any time. We never sell, share, or use your business data for any purpose outside of operating your account.
